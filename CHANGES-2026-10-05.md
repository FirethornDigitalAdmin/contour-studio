# Changes · 5 October 2026

Source backup taken before these changes: `data/source-backup-2026-10-05-pre-fixes.tar.gz`.

## Fixed

- **"A1 contains disconnected geometry"** (Sheffield with Forests on). A tile seam through a tree crown left a floating sliver narrower than the nozzle. Such slivers are now removed with a warning; larger floating geometry still fails. `backend/geometry.py: drop_cut_crumbs`.
- **"material ground … not representable as a watertight binary STL"** (multicolour cities, hexagon places). Dense colour volumes that touch themselves along edges are saved without edge welding instead of rejecting the whole pack. The tile itself is still validated strictly. `backend/export.py: colour_region_mesh`.
- **Bambu Studio projects** now use the real printer, nozzle and standard process when a Bambu Lab printer is chosen under *Your printer*, read from Bambu Studio's installed presets. Other printers get a generic profile sized for the chosen nozzle. Dual-nozzle machines (H2D, H2C, X2D) still use the generic profile. `backend/bambu.py`.
- **Tile layout was drawn upside down** compared with the print. It is now north-up, matching the assembly guide.

## New

- **Wall mounting** (`hang_mode`): none, keyhole slots, push-on wall pucks, magnetic wall pucks. Pucks are 30 mm squares fixed with one countersunk screw; the pack adds one per tile, a spacing jig and a socket fit test. Keyholes work on single maps and jigsaw trays too. `backend/mounting.py`.
- **Tile walls** grow on any side (hexagon walls not to the west), tiles can be removed, and the map shows each tile's outline.
- **Jigsaw**: round knobs now lock; classic cut lines wander like a hand-cut puzzle; land relief is scaled into a height budget while water (0.6 mm), roads (0.4 mm) and buildings (0.8 mm) are stepped; bottom edges are eased; optional row-column numbers on the back; trays wider than the bed are keyed; up to 20 × 20 pieces.
- **Frame lettering**: your own words, top or bottom edge, left/centre/right, raised or engraved, sans or serif, adjustable size. Raised lettering on a separate frame takes the special-places colour in multicolour packs.
- **Plaque**: title, second line and small print in four shapes, raised or engraved, printed with any project. `backend/plaque.py`.
- **Website**: "what you can make", mounting and finishing-touches sections with renders of real generated models. Regenerate them with `scripts/build-site-examples.py` then `scripts/render-project-examples.mjs`.

## Not physically tested

Nothing here has been printed. Before relying on it, print: `Puck_Socket_Fit` with one `Wall_puck`, the `Puzzle_Fit` pair, and a small plaque. Bambu projects were sliced with Bambu Studio's command line for an A1 with a 0.2 mm nozzle; opening them in the Studio window and sending to a printer was not checked.

## Still open

- The installed desktop app is the old build. Run from source (`start.command`) or rebuild the installers to get these changes.
- Surface detail still gets coarser as a single map gets larger (sample count is per artwork, not per mm).
- Older "legacy" collections have no wall-mounting option.
- Multicolour lettering on a built-in (integrated) frame prints in the frame colour.
